Clinical commissioning material — CQ500

The source head CT examinations are de-identified clinical images from the
CQ500 dataset released by Qure.ai with the Centre for Advanced Research in
Imaging, Neurosciences and Genomics (CARING), New Delhi. The associated study
is Chilamkurthy et al., "Deep learning algorithms for detection of critical
findings in head CT scans: a retrospective study", The Lancet, 2018.

The quantitative release-replay views include conventional and regional
attenuation-adaptive processing. They are derived images, not separate clinical
acquisitions, and retain references to their source instances and geometry.
No new patient diagnoses are implied by model scores.
