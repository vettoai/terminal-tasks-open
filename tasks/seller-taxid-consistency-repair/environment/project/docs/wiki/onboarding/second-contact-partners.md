# Partners with a second contact person

Maintained by Ivo Batista, partner-operations lead.

These partners register a separate responsible person during onboarding. The maintained list is dbo.partner_second_contact; query that table when processing an account. Registration on the individual seller must also be set. A token from one of these partners does not change the rights of a seller registered under another partner.
